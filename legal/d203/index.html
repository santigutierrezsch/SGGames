A Message to District 203

Regarding the Use of SG Games on School Property

Reference to Policy 6:235 - Access to Electronic Networks

Naperville Community Unit School District 203 Policy 6:235 (Access to Electronic Networks) establishes expectations for the appropriate use of district devices, network resources, and internet access. The policy makes clear that use of district technology is governed by school rules, may be monitored, and must remain consistent with the district's educational mission and administrative safeguards.

This page is intended to acknowledge that framework and to clarify the nature of SG Games in that context.

Among other things, the policy reflects that:

  • Use of district networks must support education, research, or legitimate school business purposes.
  • Network access is a privilege rather than a right and may be monitored by school officials.
  • Internet filtering systems are used to prevent access to content deemed obscene, pornographic, or otherwise inappropriate for students under federal law.

Those principles govern district-managed access and remain within the authority of school administrators and technology staff.

Nature of the Project

SG Games is an independently developed personal web and software project created outside district systems, accounts, and infrastructure. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, sponsored by, or operated by Naperville Community Unit School District 203 or any of its schools.

The project exists as a personal learning and experimentation environment. It was developed to practice standard technical skills and to build familiarity with the process of creating and maintaining a web-based project.

Educational Purpose

SG Games is intended primarily as an educational and developmental project involving:

  • web development
  • deployment and hosting
  • version control and project maintenance
  • user interface design
  • general programming experimentation

Its purpose is to support personal learning and technical growth, not to interfere with classroom instruction, district operations, or school technology controls.

No Circumvention and Acceptable Use

SG Games does not provide, promote, or distribute tools, instructions, or methods for bypassing school network restrictions, content filters, monitoring systems, or other district safeguards. It is not designed to evade, disable, interfere with, or undermine district technology controls.

The project is presented as an independently hosted website only. Nothing on this page should be interpreted as encouraging access in violation of school rules or as challenging the district's authority to restrict or manage access on its own systems.

User Responsibility

Any person who attempts to access SG Games while using a district device, district account, or district network remains solely responsible for complying with all applicable district and school policies, classroom rules, and administrative directions. Access to any website on district systems remains subject to school restrictions, filtering, and monitoring.

Relationship to District Policy

SG Games was created independently and outside district systems. Its existence as a personal project does not change the district's authority to regulate technology use on school property or on school-managed networks. The project acknowledges that any access within a school environment is governed by district expectations, including Policy 6:235.

For clarity, SG Games:

  • Was created outside of district systems and infrastructure.
  • Is independently developed for personal learning and experimentation.
  • Is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or operated by District 203.
  • Is not intended to interfere with the district's electronic network, monitoring practices, or educational environment.

Cooperation Statement

The creator of SG Games is willing to cooperate with school administration and to consider reasonable modifications to this page or related project materials if requested in order to better align with district expectations and address legitimate administrative concerns.

Statement to District 203

SG Games is a personal project developed independently outside district systems for learning, experimentation, and technical skill development. It is not a district resource, school-sponsored platform, or school-operated service. The intent of this statement is to communicate that the project is separate from school operations, respectful of district policy, and not meant to encourage misuse of school technology resources.
