A Message to District 203

Regarding the Use of SGGames on School Property

Reference to Policy 6:235 - Access to Electronic Networks

Naperville Community Unit School District 203 Policy 6:235 (Access to Electronic Networks) outlines expectations for the appropriate use of district electronic resources and network access. The policy states that the use of district electronic networks should be consistent with the district's educational mission and support instructional needs while maintaining appropriate online behavior.

The policy also clarifies that:

  • Use of district networks must support education, research, or legitimate school business purposes.
  • Network access is a privilege rather than a right and may be monitored by school officials.
  • Internet filtering systems are used to prevent access to content deemed obscene, pornographic, or otherwise inappropriate for students under federal law.

These provisions establish the framework through which the district manages and regulates network access and student technology use.

Nature and Purpose of SGGames

SGGames is a personal software and web development project created independently outside of school hours. The project is hosted on GitHub, a widely used platform for software development, version control, and collaborative programming.

GitHub is commonly used in educational environments, including computer science and Career and Technical Education (CTE) courses, to teach programming, software development practices, and project management skills. Hosting SGGames on GitHub reflects standard development practices used by students and professionals alike.

SGGames was developed primarily as a learning project to explore web development, programming concepts, and the deployment of web-based applications. The project itself was not created with the intention of interfering with classroom instruction, circumventing district technology safeguards, or disrupting school operations.

Compliance with District Policy

The existence of SGGames as an independently developed website does not inherently constitute a violation of District Policy 6:235. The project:

  • Was created outside of district systems and infrastructure.
  • Is hosted on a third-party development platform commonly used for educational and professional programming work.
  • Is not intended to interfere with the district's electronic network or educational environment.

Students remain responsible for complying with all district policies regarding device use, network access, and classroom expectations while on school property. If access to any particular website is restricted by district technology protections, those restrictions are acknowledged and respected.

A Message to District 203

SGGames and its associated projects are personal development work created for learning and experimentation with programming and web technologies. The project was not designed to encourage misuse of school networks or devices.

The creators of SGGames respect the policies and authority of Naperville Community Unit School District 203 and understand that access to certain websites on district networks may be filtered or restricted at the discretion of school administrators or technology staff.

SGGames is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or operated by Naperville Community Unit School District 203 or any of its schools. It exists solely as an independent personal project developed outside of school systems.

The intent of SGGames is to serve as a programming and development project for learning purposes and experimentation with web technologies, rather than as a platform designed to interfere with or circumvent school policies.
