Cookie Policy

Last Updated: March 11, 2026

1. What Are Cookies

Cookies are small text files that are stored on your device when you visit a website. They help websites remember information about your visit, such as preferences and settings.

2. How SG Games Uses Cookies

SG Games may use cookies to:

  • Ensure the website functions properly
  • Improve performance and user experience
  • Remember user preferences
  • Analyze website traffic and usage
  • Support advertising services

3. Types of Cookies We May Use

Essential Cookies

These cookies are required for the basic operation of the website. Without them, certain features may not function properly.

Performance and Analytics Cookies

These cookies help us understand how visitors interact with the website, such as which pages are visited most often.

Advertising Cookies

Third-party advertising networks may use cookies to show relevant advertisements and measure the effectiveness of ads.

4. Third-Party Cookies

Some cookies may be placed by third-party services used on the website, such as analytics providers, embedded games, or advertising partners. These services have their own privacy policies governing their use of data.

5. Managing Cookies

Most web browsers allow you to control or disable cookies through their settings. You can choose to block or delete cookies at any time, but doing so may affect how the website functions.

6. Changes to This Cookie Policy

We may update this Cookie Policy from time to time. Updates will be posted on this page with the revised date.

7. Contact

If you have any questions about our use of cookies, you may contact us through the contact page on this website.
